What is Fitronix?

Fitronix is a compact, user-friendly system that measures heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO₂), and temperature using reliable sensors and AI-assisted signal processing. It displays clear, real-time readings on an LCD and provides alerts and guidance for quick, at-home health checks.

How it works

The device captures raw signals from onboard sensors, applies filtering and smoothing to reduce noise, and calculates vital metrics like heart rate and SpO₂. A program coded for an Arduino microcontroller handles sampling, processing, and updates the LCD in real time.

How to use the device

  • Power On — Press the power button at the back. The LCD shows a welcome message, then intro slides.
  • Heart Rate & SpO₂ — When it says “Place Finger”, insert your finger into the right opening and hold still.
  • Pulse — When prompted, insert your finger into the left opening and stay steady.
  • Temperature — When it says “Hold the temp”, place the steel probe on your hand or surface.
  • Restart — After the thank-you screen, long-press to power off, then press again to restart.

Components

  1. Black & Yellow Box: HR, SpO₂ & Pulse
  2. Red & Brown Box: Main Processor
  3. Blue Rectangle: LCD Display
  4. One Wire: Temperature Sensor
  5. RTC (DS3231): Accurate time even after power-off with a coin cell
  6. 10,000 mAh Power Bank: Portable supply; about one month of daily use per charge
  7. Arduino Uno Clone: Device brain
  8. Buzzer: Smart beeps
  9. MAX30100: Heart rate and SpO₂
  10. Pulse analog sensor: Pulse on LCD
